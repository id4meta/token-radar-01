How the implied value is worked out

Every protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot: no symbol, no CoinGecko id, no market cap. The implied value is the board's own claim, never the snapshot's: implied value = 8% × TVLeff × Mcat × Wcat × Wgrowth × Wage × Wchain × Wwl × Wsib.

  • TVLeff — TVL compressed sub-linearly, 50M × (TVL / 50M)^0.65, so a $7B bridge does not drown out a $30M early protocol.
  • Mcat — the median market-cap/TVL of the snapshot's own tokenized protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median (0.2138 here).
  • Wcat — how often the category launches a token: native DeFi (lending, DEXs, perps) scores high, bridges and wrapped products score low.
  • Wgrowth — 7-day TVL change measured against the pre-token median (−5.48% this snapshot), winsorized so one wild week cannot decide the board.
  • Wage — the farming window peaks 1–12 months after listing and fades past 900 days with no token.
  • Wchain — how fresh and deep the protocol's best chain's pre-token ecosystem is, with a small multi-chain bonus.
  • Wwl / Wsib — 1.35× when the snapshot's own watchlist names the protocol; 0.35× when it looks like a sibling product of a protocol whose token already trades.

A protocol on the watchlist records no token — it does not prove it has none. Farm plans are generic steps derived from the protocol's category, chains, TVL and age; they are not investment advice, and no token launch is guaranteed.
